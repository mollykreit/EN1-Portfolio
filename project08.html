Project 8: Haunted House

For this project, we teamed up to design spooky robots for a haunted house in the engineering makerspace on campus. My partner and I initially wanted to drop either a ghost or candy on people who passed by. We first planned to make the drop random, then revised the idea so the outcome would depend on a game.

Our final design had a LEGO hub on a table, with cutouts for the hub and two force sensors. The game played a random sequence of three to eight pumpkins or ghosts, and the user repeated it by pressing the matching symbol on each force sensor. We changed the start control from tapping the hub to pressing a force sensor so a bump to the table would not start the game. We also added feedback to show what the player pressed and whether it was correct, plus a cooldown between games.

The other part of the design was a drop mechanism mounted overhead. One side used a motor to raise and lower candy, while the other used a clutch to drop a ghost. We made a plywood platform to hang the mechanism from the ceiling after having trouble balancing it, and reinforced the motors because both objects were heavy.

We also worked through challenges with communication between the table and ceiling hubs, and getting the drop mechanism to release when the axle kept getting pinched. Overall, we had a lot of fun making the design and enjoyed watching people interact with it.
