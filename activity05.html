Activity 5: Simple Car

For this project, we built a simple car and programmed it to spin around, travel in an arc, etc. to get a feel for micropython and how to use the wheels to move the car how we wanted. To accomplish this, we learned how to pair motors, change their speed to control the car's movement, and manipulate code in other ways. We also used the drop test on our cars to make see how structural they were. Finally, I worked on other challenges like making the robot turn when it meets a wall and attempting to make it self-balancing (which is still in the process, currently needs some support).
