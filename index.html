Project 1: Getting to Know You

We designed builds that represent a part of ourselves as an introduction activity. An added challenge was making a tensegrity structure. I made a tensegrity fish tank with 2 fish because of my love of marine life.

Project 2: Silly Walks

We each had 15 minutes during class to create a robot that walks/moves across the room without using wheels or other spinning objects.

Project 3: Biomimicry

Our 3rd project was inspired by biomimicry. We designed a robot that mimics the structure and movement of an animal of our choosing. I chose to replicate the slithering motion of a snake. Some of the challenges I faced were converted circular motion to linear motion, and getting gear ratios to work out in my favor. One revision I made was anchoring the middle segment in place to minimize the otherwise odd/unpredictable movement.

Project 4: Spirograph

We designed and programmed a robot to create spirograph drawings by controlling the motors, marker arms, and rotating paper.

Activity 1: Design a Coaster

We used generative AI to design a drink coaster for a specific person of our choosing. We selected Sherlock Holmes. Our design centered around his pipe.

Activity 2: Object Redesign

We were challenged to redesign an object to fit a given modifier. We were tasked to make a small/tiny lightbulb/light source. We came up with decorative fairy lights using fireflies.

Activity 3: Paper Airplane

We each asked generative AI to give us step-by-step instructions on how to build a paper airplane. We could only follow the instructions directly; we weren't allowed to use any prior knowledge

Activity 4: Top Spinner

For this activity, we used what we learned during a lesson about gears to build a top and a mechanism to release an spin it. By gearing down and putting gears in sequence, we were able to get our tops spinning much faster than if we only used the motor.
