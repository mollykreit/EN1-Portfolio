Activity 8: High or Low

In this activity, we learned about machine learning, specifically nearest-neighbor classification. I built a device that uses this method to predict whether an object is short or tall. When the force sensor is pressed during data collection, the device records an ultrasonic distance reading with a motor position that indicates whether the example is high or low. After a button on the hub switches the device to prediction mode, a new ultrasonic reading is compared with the recorded examples, and the motor moves to a position representing the predicted class.
